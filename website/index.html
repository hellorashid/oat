About

I wanted something like Granola and the other meeting-note apps, without living in someone else’s workspace. Oat just records and transcribes locally. Bring your own agent harness, markdown editor, and workflow — then drop meetings into the tools you already use, instead of another app that’s only for recording.

  • Records your mic and meeting audio on this Mac
  • Transcribes locally into markdown
  • Saves files in a folder you choose
  • No account, no cloud, no extra workspace
